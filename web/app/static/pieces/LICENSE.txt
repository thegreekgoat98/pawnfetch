Chess piece images
==================

The SVG files in this folder are the "Cburnett" chess pieces by Colin M.L. Burnett,
from Wikimedia Commons, e.g. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chess_klt45.svg

The author offers them under a choice of licences (GFDL, CC BY-SA 3.0, BSD, GPL).
pawnfetch uses them under the BSD licence below.

Changes made: renamed (e.g. Chess_klt45.svg -> wK.svg), and the fixed width/height
replaced with viewBox="0 0 45 45" so they scale with the board.
